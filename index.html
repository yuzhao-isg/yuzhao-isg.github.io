Yu Zhao

PhD Candidate in Public Health · Environmental Epidemiology

Pompeu Fabra University & Barcelona Institute for Global Health (ISGlobal)

I am a predoctoral researcher integrating environmental exposure assessment, statistical and machine-learning modeling, and molecular epidemiology. My research examines how prenatal heat exposure affects fetal brain development and postnatal neurodevelopment, with investigation into placental hemodynamics and DNA methylation as potential pathways. This work is supported by the European Research Council.
